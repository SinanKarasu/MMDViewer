# MMDViewer Privacy Policy

MMDViewer for iPad and Mac opens Mathpix Markdown documents you choose. It renders them on your device, including when you use Quick Look on Mac. MMDViewer does not send your documents to the developer or to Mathpix for rendering.

The app does not require an account or collect personal data. It does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics. The developer does not receive or retain a copy of your documents. You can manage or delete your files in Files on iPad or Finder on Mac.

If a document references an online resource, loading it can contact the site that hosts it. If you open a web link or use a cloud file provider, that service handles your activity under its own privacy policy.

For privacy questions, use [MMDViewer support](https://github.com/SinanKarasu/MMDViewer/issues). GitHub issues are public.
